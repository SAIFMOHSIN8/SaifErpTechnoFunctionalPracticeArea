ticket: P2P cycle implementation

objective
complete the Oracle EBS Procure-to-Pay cycle from creating a purchase request through paying the supplier and transferring the accounting entries to General Ledger.

transaction details
- item: XX_MACBOOK_SAIF
- requisition: 16113
- purchase order: 6762
- receipt: 9141
- invoice: SAIF-INV-6762
- invoice amount: USD 900
- transfer to GL request: 7579655
- journal import request: 7579656

process flow
item -> requisition -> purchase order -> receipt -> invoice -> payment -> accounting -> general ledger

1. verify the item
responsibility:
Inventory Vision Operations (USA)

navigation:
Items -> Master Items

- queried item XX_MACBOOK_SAIF.
- confirmed that it was assigned to the required organization.
- verified that it was purchasable and transactable.

2. create and approve the requisition
responsibility:
Purchasing Vision Operations (USA)

navigation:
Requisitions -> Requisitions

- entered item XX_MACBOOK_SAIF.
- entered the quantity, destination, organization, and charge account.
- saved requisition 16113.
- submitted it for approval.
- confirmed that its status was Approved.

3. create and approve the purchase order
- converted the approved requisition into a standard purchase order.
- confirmed the supplier, item, quantity, price, shipment, and distribution.
- saved purchase order 6762.
- submitted it for approval.
- verified that the PO status was Approved.

4. receive the item
navigation:
Receiving -> Receipts

- searched using PO 6762.
- selected the outstanding shipment.
- entered the received quantity and receiving details.
- saved the transaction.
- Oracle generated receipt 9141.
- confirmed that the goods reached their final destination.

5. create and match the AP invoice
responsibility:
Payables Vision Operations (USA)

navigation:
Invoices -> Entry -> Invoices

- entered invoice SAIF-INV-6762.
- entered an invoice amount of USD 900.
- matched the invoice to PO 6762 and receipt 9141.
- confirmed that the invoice distributions totaled USD 900.
- saved the invoice.

three-way match
- ordered: PO 6762
- received: receipt 9141
- invoiced: SAIF-INV-6762

6. validate and account the invoice
from the invoice:
Actions -> Validate

- validated the invoice.
- confirmed that there were no unresolved holds.

from the invoice:
Actions -> Create Accounting

- selected Final accounting.
- confirmed that the invoice accounting status was Accounted.

7. pay the supplier
working navigation:
Invoice -> Scheduled Payments -> Pay...

- confirmed that the amount remaining was USD 900.
- confirmed that the payment method was CHECK.
- verified that no payment hold existed.
- created a Quick Payment.
- selected bank account BofA-204.
- selected Global_payment process profile.
- completed the USD 900 payment.

8. account the payment
from the completed payment:
Actions -> Create Accounting

- selected Final accounting.
- submitted the accounting process.
- confirmed that the payment accounting status was Processed.

9. transfer the entries to General Ledger
navigation:
View -> Requests -> Submit a New Request

- submitted Transfer Journal Entries to GL.
- request 7579655 completed normally.
- the process launched Journal Import request 7579656.
- Journal Import also completed with a Normal status.

10. verify the GL journal
responsibility:
General Ledger Vision Operations (USA)

navigation:
Journals -> Enter

- searched for journals with the Payables source.
- located the imported Purchase Invoices and Payments journals.
- reviewed the journal lines and account combinations.
- confirmed that total debits equaled total credits.

the reviewed payment journal showed:
- debit: USD 3,900
- credit: USD 3,900
- result: Balanced

the journal total was greater than the individual USD 900 payment because Oracle summarized multiple Payables transactions in the imported GL journal.

important checks
- the requisition and PO must be approved.
- the PO must have an outstanding quantity before receiving.
- transaction and GL dates must fall within open accounting periods.
- invoice quantity and price must agree with the PO and receipt.
- invoice holds must be investigated and corrected.
- invoice and payment accounting must be Final.
- both the transfer and Journal Import requests must complete normally.

result
the full P2P cycle was completed successfully. The item was requested, ordered, received, invoiced, paid, accounted, transferred to General Ledger, and verified in a balanced GL journal.

scope note
opening stock, RFQ, quotation, and detailed SLA SQL tracing were optional exercises and were not required for the completed P2P cycle.
